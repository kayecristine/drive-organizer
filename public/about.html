What Nexus Drive Organizer Does

Google Drive is powerful, but over time it becomes cluttered with thousands of loose, unsorted files. Nexus Drive Organizer solves this by providing three core tools that work directly with your Google Drive data:

📂

Manual File Triage

Browse all your unsorted Google Drive files in a clean, sortable interface. Select files individually or in bulk, then drag and drop them into any folder or use the quick-move panel. Full visibility over your entire Drive.

🔍

Duplicate File Scanner

Scans your Google Drive to identify files that share the same name and file size — a reliable indicator of exact duplicates. Review matched pairs side-by-side and permanently delete the ones you don't need to reclaim storage.

🤖

AI Auto-Pilot Organizer

Connect your own AI API key (e.g., Google Gemini or OpenAI). The extension reads your file names and MIME types, sends them to the AI, and receives smart folder groupings. It then automatically creates folders and moves files — entirely within your Google Drive.

Why This App Needs Google Drive Access

To perform file organization, Nexus Drive Organizer requires access to your Google Drive through the Google Drive API. Below is a precise explanation of every permission requested and exactly why it is necessary for the application to function.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive

Full Google Drive Access

This permission is required because the application needs to: (1) list all files in your Drive to populate the File Manager and Duplicate Scanner; (2) read file metadata (name, MIME type, size, parent folder) to power the AI categorizer and duplicate detection; (3) create new folders in your Drive when the AI Auto-Pilot groups files; and (4) move files between folders when you triage them manually or when the AI Auto-Pilot reorganizes them. All four actions are core functions of the extension and cannot be performed with a narrower scope.

Our Data & Privacy Principles

We believe in full transparency about how your data is used. Here is exactly what happens when you use Nexus Drive Organizer:

  • ✓
    All processing is local. Your Google Drive file list is fetched directly from the Google Drive API into your browser. No file data is routed through or stored on any Nexus server.
  • ✓
    We do not store your files or metadata. Nexus Drive Organizer does not operate any backend database. When you close the extension, your Drive data is cleared from memory.
  • ✓
    AI features use your own API key. If you use the AI Auto-Pilot, you provide your own API key. File names and types are sent directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider (e.g., Google Gemini). Nexus never sees or stores your API key or your files.
  • ✓
    We do not sell or share your data. No personal information, Google account data, or Drive metadata is sold, rented, or shared with any third party for any purpose.
  • ✓
    Authentication is handled by Google. Sign-in is performed entirely through Google's official Chrome Identity API. OAuth tokens are managed by your Chrome browser and are never transmitted to Nexus servers.

How It Works

Nexus Drive Organizer is a Chrome Extension — it runs entirely inside your Chrome browser as an extension popup. There is no separate website or web app to log into. After installing from the Chrome Web Store, you click the extension icon in your browser toolbar, sign in with your Google Account, and begin organizing your Drive immediately.

The extension communicates directly and exclusively with the Google Drive API on your behalf using the OAuth 2.0 token issued by your Chrome browser. All file organization actions (moving, creating folders, deleting duplicates) are performed through official Google Drive API calls.
