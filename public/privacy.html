Privacy Policy for Nexus File Manager

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Nexus File Manager ("we," "our," or "the Extension") is a Chrome Extension designed to help you organize and manage your Google Drive files. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and how it is secured.

2. Data We Access

To function correctly, Nexus File Manager requires access to your Google Drive via the Google Drive API. We specifically request the minimum necessary permissions to read, move, and organize your files and folders.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Local Processing: All file organization, sorting, and categorization happen entirely within your local browser.
  • No External Storage: We do NOT transmit, store, or save your Google Drive files, file metadata, or personal information on any external servers or third-party databases.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

4. Authentication and Security

The Extension uses Google's official Chrome Identity API for authentication. Your OAuth tokens are managed securely by your Chrome browser and are never transmitted to our servers (because we don't have any!).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer directly.
